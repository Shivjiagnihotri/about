A LITTLE CONTEXT FOR THE COLLECTION

Open doors.
Traceable sources.

The Learning Garden combines an editorial collection with a larger index of open learning materials. It is a personal project by Shivji Agnihotri, created to make machine learning easier to explore and share.

Two ways to explore

The visual concepts, bookshelf, and recommended paths are selected for clarity and learning value. The full library goes wider: individual tutorials, lessons, reading material, and research records gathered from official sites and maintained open collections. An indexed entry is a place to explore, not a claim that every learner needs it.

Where the links come from

Books link to their authors, authorized publishers, universities, or official repositories. Tutorials and course lessons are collected from original providers. Research records use published open metadata and link to the original paper. Each card includes its provider and, where useful, the source used to discover it.

  • Book-by-book source and access notes
  • Selected courses, guides, and official access checks
  • Current library counts and import date
  • Catalogue data and collection notes on GitHub
  • The scripts used to refresh the collection

What “free” means here

The linked reading, videos, documentation, or research papers can be accessed without purchasing the material. Some providers also offer paid certificates, optional cloud compute, or commercial products. Those services are separate from the free learning links. A small number of older author sites use HTTP; they are retained as primary sources rather than replaced with unofficial copies.

Checks, dates, and limitations

The curated links were reviewed in October 2026. The larger index records its import date and provenance in the collection notes. Verification varies by source: a resource may be directly checked by HTTP, discovered in an official sitemap, or confirmed through an open research metadata record. Links can move, and a successful page response does not assess teaching quality.

Research papers may be preprints and may not have been peer reviewed. “Advanced” reflects the mathematical reading expected of research, while “Not level-specific” means the source does not assign a level. Topic labels are browsing aids and may not cover every subject within a resource.

Video previews

Video cards use preview art supplied by the original course or video page, with a garden illustration when no usable image is available. Covers open the lessons on their original host. The creators retain ownership of their preview images; image sources and dates are recorded with the collection.

Made to be shared

Use “Copy this collection link” to share the library with its search and filters. Each concept also has its own shareable address. Hearts save items in your browser on this device; they are not uploaded or included in shared links. The interactive examples run locally and use small, explained datasets.

Help the garden grow

Found a broken link, a misleading label, or a useful open resource? Suggest a correction on GitHub. Original books, courses, and papers remain with their authors. This site indexes links and metadata; it does not rehost their contents.
